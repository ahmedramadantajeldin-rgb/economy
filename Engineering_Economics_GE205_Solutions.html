Annual Worth — Decreasing Arithmetic Gradient القيمة السنوية — تسلسل هندسي حسابي تنازلي

Ch. 6 · AW
Problem: Find the annual worth of the following cash flows at i = 12%/year, n = 6 years:
Payments (end of year): Year 1 = $800, Year 2 = $700, Year 3 = $600, Year 4 = $500, Year 5 = $400, Year 6 = $300.
📊 Cash Flow Diagram
Given: Abase = $800  |  G = $100 (decreasing)  |  i = 12%/yr  |  n = 6 yrs  |  Find: AW = ?
💡

المفهوم: نفصّل السلسلة إلى جزأين: قيمة ثابتة Abase = $800 ناقص تسلسل تصاعدي G = $100. المعادلة: AW = Abase − G × (A/G, i%, n)

Formula — Decreasing Arithmetic Gradient
AW = Abase − G × (A/G, i%, n)
📋 Table Factors at i = 12%, n = 6
  • (A/G, 12%, 6) = 2.1720
AW = Abase − G × (A/G, 12%, 6) = 800 − 100 × (2.1720)
= 800 − 217.20
AW = $582.80 / year

Capital Recovery & Capitalized Cost تكلفة استرداد رأس المال — التكلفة الرأسمالية الدائمة

Ch. 6 · CR & CC

Part (a) — Capital Recovery of a Milling Machine

A milling machine: P = $85,000, S = $15,000 after n = 10 years.
Annual revenue = $18,000/yr. O&M costs: A₁ = $1,800/yr increasing by G = $500/yr. i = 10%.
📊 Cash Flow Diagram — Milling Machine
Given: P = $85,000  |  S = $15,000  |  n = 10 yrs  |  i = 10%  |  Revenue = $18,000/yr  |  AOC₁ = $1,800  |  G = $500/yr
📋 Table Factors at i = 10%, n = 10
  • (A/P, 10%, 10) = 0.1627
  • (A/F, 10%, 10) = 0.0627
  • (A/G, 10%, 10) = 3.7255
Step 1 — Capital Recovery (CR): CR = P × (A/P, 10%, 10) − S × (A/F, 10%, 10)
   = 85,000 × 0.1627 − 15,000 × 0.0627
   = 13,829.50 − 940.50 = $12,889/yr
Step 2 — Equivalent Annual O&M (Increasing Gradient): AOCequiv = A₁ + G × (A/G, 10%, 10)
          = 1,800 + 500 × 3.7255
          = 1,800 + 1,862.75 = $3,662.75/yr
Step 3 — Annual Worth (Profitability Check): AW = Revenue − CR − AOCequiv
   = 18,000 − 12,889 − 3,662.75 = +$1,448.25/yr ✓ (profitable)
CR = $12,889/yr  |  AW = +$1,448/yr

Part (b) — Capitalized Cost of a Perpetual Series

Find the Capitalized Cost of $10,000 every 5 years forever, starting year 5, at i = 12%/year.
Given: Arep = $10,000 (every 5 yrs)  |  ncycle = 5 yrs  |  i = 12%  |  Find: CC = ?
Capitalized Cost — Perpetual Periodic Payments
CC = Arep / [(1 + i)n − 1]
CC = 10,000 / [(1.12)⁵ − 1]
= 10,000 / [1.7623 − 1]
= 10,000 / 0.7623 = $13,117
CC ≈ $13,117

PW & FW — Two Different Interest Rates القيمة الحالية والمستقبلية — معدلا فائدة مختلفان

Ch. 3+5 · Combining Factors
Cash flows: $30/yr at years 3, 4, 5  |  $50/yr at years 6, 7, 8  (all payments are costs ↓)
i₁ = 8%/yr for years 0 → 5  |  i₂ = 10%/yr for years 5 → 8
Find: (a) PW at year 0  |  (b) FW at year 8
📊 Cash Flow Diagram
Given: A₁ = $30/yr (yrs 3,4,5)  |  A₂ = $50/yr (yrs 6,7,8)  |  i₁ = 8% (yrs 0→5)  |  i₂ = 10% (yrs 5→8)
🔑

الاستراتيجية:
• PW: كل مجموعة → قيمتها عند بداية فترتها → ثم نخصم إلى yr 0 بمعدل i₁ = 8%.
• FW: كل مجموعة → قيمتها عند نهاية فترتها → ثم نجمع عند yr 8.

📋 Table Factors
  • (P/A, 8%, 3) = 2.5771
  • (P/F, 8%, 2) = 0.8573
  • (P/F, 8%, 5) = 0.6806
  • (P/A, 10%, 3) = 2.4869
  • (F/A, 8%, 3) = 3.2464
  • (F/P, 10%, 3) = 1.3310
  • (F/A, 10%, 3) = 3.3100
Part (a) — PW at Year 0: Group 1 — $30/yr at yrs 3, 4, 5 (i₁ = 8%):
  PW at yr 2  = 30 × (P/A, 8%, 3)  = 30 × 2.5771  = $77.31
  PW at yr 0  = 77.31 × (P/F, 8%, 2) = 77.31 × 0.8573 = $66.27

Group 2 — $50/yr at yrs 6, 7, 8 (i₂ = 10%):
  PW at yr 5  = 50 × (P/A, 10%, 3) = 50 × 2.4869  = $124.35
  PW at yr 0  = 124.35 × (P/F, 8%, 5) = 124.35 × 0.6806 = $84.64

PWtotal = 66.27 + 84.64 = $150.91
PW ≈ $150.91
Part (b) — FW at Year 8: Group 1 — $30/yr (yrs 3,4,5) → FW at yr 5, then grow to yr 8 at i₂=10%:
  FW at yr 5 = 30 × (F/A, 8%, 3)  = 30 × 3.2464  = $97.39
  FW at yr 8 = 97.39 × (F/P, 10%, 3) = 97.39 × 1.3310 = $129.63

Group 2 — $50/yr (yrs 6,7,8) at i₂=10%:
  FW at yr 8 = 50 × (F/A, 10%, 3) = 50 × 3.3100 = $165.50

FWtotal = 129.63 + 165.50 = $295.13
✓ Cross-check: PW × (F/P,8%,5) × (F/P,10%,3) = 150.91 × 1.4693 × 1.3310 ≈ $295.13 ✓
PW = $150.91  |  FW = $295.13

Ch. 4 · Nominal vs. Effective Rate
Determine the present worth of the following cash flows at an interest rate of 16% per year compounded semiannually.
EOY012345
$ (Cash Flow) 00 21,000 24,000 0 10,000
📊 Cash Flow Diagram — Annual (EOY 0–5)
Given: Nominal i = 16%/yr, compounded semiannually  |  Payments at EOY 2, 3, 5  |  Find: PW at yr 0 = ?
💡

التحويل إلى الفترات النصف سنوية:
isemi = 16% ÷ 2 = 8% per 6-month period

Replacement Study — Insider's vs. Outsider's Viewpoint دراسة الاستبدال — المنظور الداخلي مقابل المنظور الخارجي

Ch. 11 · Replacement Analysis
Defender (Existing Machine): Purchased 5 yrs ago for $75,000. Current MV = $35,000. AOC = $12,000/yr. SV after 3 yrs = $20,000.
Challenger (New Machine): P = $45,000. AOC = $9,000/yr. SV after 3 yrs = $25,000.
i = 10%, n = 3 years for both.

Part (a) — Cash Flow Diagrams: Insider's Viewpoint

📌

المنظور الداخلي (Insider's): نتجاهل الـ Sunk Cost ($75,000 السعر الأصلي). المدافع لا يحتاج استثمار جديد عند السنة 0.

🛡 Defender — Insider's Viewpoint
Yr 0:  $0  (no investment — keep it)
Yrs 1–3: −$12,000/yr  (AOC)
Yr 3:  +$20,000  (SV)
$75,000 original cost = SUNK COST → IGNORE
⚔️ Challenger — Insider's Viewpoint
Yr 0: −$45,000 (buy) + $35,000 (sell defender)
       = −$10,000 net
Yrs 1–3: −$9,000/yr  (AOC)
Yr 3:  +$25,000  (SV)
📊 CFD — Defender (Outsider's Viewpoint: P = MV = $35,000)

Part (b) — AW Analysis: Outsider's Viewpoint

👁️

المنظور الخارجي (Outsider's): نعامل MV = $35,000 كأنه التكلفة الأولى للمدافع (تكلفة الفرصة البديلة).

📋 Table Factors at i = 10%, n = 3
  • (P/A, 10%, 3) = 2.4869
  • (P/F, 10%, 3) = 0.7513
PW(Defender) — P = MV = $35,000:
= −35,000 − 12,000 × (P/A,10%,3) + 20,000 × (P/F,10%,3)
= −35,000 − 12,000 × 2.4869 + 20,000 × 0.7513
= −35,000 − 29,843 + 15,026 = −$49,817
PW(Challenger) — P = $45,000:
= −45,000 − 9,000 × (P/A,10%,3) + 25,000 × (P/F,10%,3)
= −45,000 − 9,000 × 2.4869 + 25,000 × 0.7513
= −45,000 − 22,382 + 18,783 = −$48,599
AlternativePW ($)Decision
Defender−$49,817—
Challenger−$48,599✓ Selected
🔄
Decision: Replace — Select the Challenger.
PW(Challenger) = −$48,599 > PW(Defender) = −$49,817 → Challenger saves ~$1,218 in PW terms.

Capitalized Cost & Capital Recovery التكلفة الرأسمالية — تكلفة استرداد رأس المال

Ch. 5+6 · CC & CR

Part (a) — Capitalized Cost of a Dam (i = 12%)

Dam: P = $2,000,000. Annual operating cost = $5,000/yr. Periodic maintenance = $10,000 every 5 years forever. i = 12%.
Capitalized Cost — Three Components
CC = P + (AOC / i) + Arep / [(1+i)n − 1]
CC₁ — Initial Construction:
CC₁ = P = $2,000,000
CC₂ — Annual O&M (forever):
CC₂ = AOC / i = 5,000 / 0.12 = $41,667
CC₃ — Periodic Maintenance (every 5 yrs, forever):
CC₃ = 10,000 / [(1.12)⁵ − 1] = 10,000 / [1.7623 − 1] = 10,000 / 0.7623 = $13,117
CCtotal = 2,000,000 + 41,667 + 13,117 = $2,054,784
CC ≈ $2,054,784

Part (b) — Capital Recovery of a Milling Machine (i = 8%)

Milling machine: P = $285,000, S = $50,000 after 10 years.
Annual revenue = $52,000/yr. AOC starts at $10,000 increasing by G = $1,000/yr. i = 8%.
📋 Table Factors at i = 8%, n = 10
  • (A/P, 8%, 10) = 0.1490
  • (A/F, 8%, 10) = 0.0690
  • (A/G, 8%, 10) = 3.8713
Step 1 — Capital Recovery (CR):
CR = P×(A/P,8%,10) − S×(A/F,8%,10)
   = 285,000×0.1490 − 50,000×0.0690
   = 42,465 − 3,450 = $39,015/yr
Step 2 — Equivalent Annual O&M (Gradient):
AOCequiv = 10,000 + 1,000×(A/G,8%,10)
          = 10,000 + 1,000×3.8713
          = 10,000 + 3,871 = $13,871/yr
Step 3 — AW Check:
AW = 52,000 − 39,015 − 13,871 = −$886/yr (slightly unprofitable at i = 8%)
CR = $39,015/yr  |  AW = −$886/yr

PW, FW & Equivalent Interest Rate — Two Segments القيمة الحالية والمستقبلية — معدل الفائدة المكافئ لقطاعين

Ch. 4+5 · PW · FW · ieq
Cash flows: $100/yr at years 1–5 at i₁ = 10%, then $160/yr at years 6–8 at i₂ = 12%.
Find: (a) PW at year 0  |  (b) FW at year 8  |  (c) Equivalent single interest rate ieq
📊 Cash Flow Diagram — Two Segments
📋 Table Factors
  • (P/A, 10%, 5) = 3.7908
  • (P/A, 12%, 3) = 2.4018
  • (P/F, 10%, 5) = 0.6209
  • (F/A, 10%, 5) = 6.1051
  • (F/P, 12%, 3) = 1.4049
  • (F/A, 12%, 3) = 3.3744
Part (a) — PW at Year 0:

Segment 1 ($100/yr, yrs 1–5, i₁=10%):
PW₁ = 100 × (P/A, 10%, 5) = 100 × 3.7908 = $379.08

Segment 2 ($160/yr, yrs 6–8, i₂=12%): bring to yr 5 first, then to yr 0:
  PW at yr 5 = 160 × (P/A, 12%, 3) = 160 × 2.4018 = $384.29
  PW at yr 0 = 384.29 × (P/F, 10%, 5) = 384.29 × 0.6209 = $238.62

PWtotal = 379.08 + 238.62 = $617.70
PW = $617.70
Part (b) — FW at Year 8:

Segment 1: Compound $100/yr to yr 5, then grow to yr 8:
  F at yr 5 = 100 × (F/A, 10%, 5) = 100 × 6.1051 = $610.51
  F at yr 8 = 610.51 × (F/P, 12%, 3) = 610.51 × 1.4049 = $857.72

Segment 2: $160/yr at yrs 6–8 at i₂=12%:
  F at yr 8 = 160 × (F/A, 12%, 3) = 160 × 3.3744 = $539.91

FWtotal = 857.72 + 539.91 = $1,397.63
FW = $1,397.63
Part (c) — Equivalent Single Interest Rate (ieq):

Using: FW = PW × (1 + ieq)⁸
(1 + ieq)⁸ = FW / PW = 1,397.63 / 617.70 = 2.2627
ieq = (2.2627)^(1/8) − 1 = 1.1075 − 1 = 0.1075 = 10.75%

✓ Verification: 617.70 × (1.1075)⁸ = $1,397.63
PW = $617.70  |  FW = $1,397.63  |  ieq = 10.75%
